# Your First Tasks

Once Row-Bot can think (see [First Launch](/docs/getting-started/first-launch)), try these from **New chat**. Each one shows a different part of Row-Bot. Type them in your own words: you don't need to name a feature.

## 1. Plan Something

> "I want to redo my small garden this spring on a £300 budget. Lay out what I should do and when."

Row-Bot may first ask a question or two in a card. Then it makes a **page** beside the chat, a plan you can approve, change or skip step by step. **Send to Row-Bot** sends your decisions back. See [Pages](/docs/chat/#pages).

## 2. Ask About Today

> "What's the weather in Leeds today?"

Anything that changes, such as weather, news or prices, is looked up live and never guessed. The step shows what Row-Bot checked.

## 3. Let Row-Bot Remember

> "By the way, I'm vegetarian and my partner is called Sam."

Row-Bot saves both and says so. In a new chat, ask "What should I cook tonight?" and it uses what it knows. See what it remembers in **Home › Knowledge**, or keep a chat out of memory with [incognito](/docs/chat/#incognito-chats).

## 4. Set Something Up To Run By Itself

> "Every weekday at 8am I'd like a short summary of the BBC technology headlines."

Row-Bot creates a [workflow](/docs/guides/workflows) and shows its steps. Find it in **Home › Workflows**, where **Run** tries it now. A reminder works the same way: "Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 10."

## 5. Use It From Your Phone

Open **Settings › Devices & remote access › Connect a phone or computer** and scan the code. Your phone gets the same chats, approvals and pages. See [Phone And Native Desktop](/docs/mobile-native/).
